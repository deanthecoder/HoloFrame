Utah Teapot, 2026 version by Cem Yuksel.
Based on the historic Utah Teapot developed at the University of Utah.

Source: https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/teapot/
Downloaded as a y-up triangle mesh at resolution 16, with the round bottom,
interior, trimmed handle and spout, and curvature-continuous surfaces.

The University of Utah Graphics Lab makes the model files freely available for
any use, including commercial use. Its terms of use and attribution request
are published at the source URL above. The model is provided as is, without
warranty.
